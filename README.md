# 🇺🇸 Brazilian Federal Government Travel Expenses Dashboard

An interactive dashboard analyzing official travel expenses from Brazil's Federal Government in 2025, built with Python, Pandas, Plotly and Streamlit.

🔗 **[Live Demo](https://painel-de-viagens-oficiais.streamlit.app)**

📁 Source Code: https://github.com/Danjunq87/analise-viagens-governo-2025

## Key Insights

- 📊 Over **807,000 official trips** were analyzed across **207 government agencies**
- 💰 Total expenditure reached **R$ 19.96 billion** in 2025
- 🏛️ The highest-spending agency was the **Ministry of Justice and Public Security**, totaling R$ 2.33 billion
- 🔒 **54.84% of total spending** came from trips classified as confidential or unidentified — raising transparency concerns
- 📍 Nearly **37,000 different destinations** were recorded, showing wide geographic dispersion
- 🧹 6,387 missing values were identified and treated, demonstrating real-world data cleaning practices

# 🇧🇷 Dashboard de Viagens Oficiais do Governo Federal

Dashboard interativo para análise dos gastos com viagens oficiais do Governo Federal Brasileiro em 2025.

## Principais Descobertas

- 📊 Mais de **807 mil viagens** analisadas em **207 órgãos**
- 💰 Total de **R$ 19,96 bilhões** em despesas
- 🏛️ Maior gasto: **Ministério da Justiça e Segurança Pública** — R$ 2,33 bilhões
- 🔒 **54,84% dos gastos** classificados como sigilo ou não identificados
- 📍 Quase **37 mil destinos diferentes** registrados
- 🧹 6.387 valores ausentes identificados e tratados
